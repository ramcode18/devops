ABOUT HARMONYCLASS

Learn, Practice and Grow Together

HarmonyClass is a digital music learning platform designed to connect music teachers and students.

Teachers can upload music exercises, share audio demonstrations, assign activities and provide feedback on student performances.

Students can access lessons, submit their practice recordings and track their musical progress.
